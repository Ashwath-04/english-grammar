Each table shows a **wrong** sentence, the **right** one, and **why**. The heading of each group links to the full lesson. These are the mistakes that come up most often in exams, letters and essays.

## [Verb forms and auxiliaries](/foundation/f2-verb-forms/)

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *He has ~~went~~ home.* | *He has **gone** home.* | After *has / have / had*, use V3. |
| *I ~~seen~~ that film.* | *I **saw** / **have seen** that film.* | V3 cannot stand alone as the main verb. |
| *He ~~studys~~ hard.* | *He **studies** hard.* | Consonant + *y* → *-ies*. |
| *The bus ~~stoped~~.* | *The bus **stopped**.* | One vowel + one consonant: double the consonant. |
| *Prices have ~~raised~~.* | *Prices have **risen**.* | *Rise* (no object) – *rose* – *risen*. *Raise* needs an object. |
| *I ~~laid~~ down for a rest.* | *I **lay** down for a rest.* | *Lie* (rest) – *lay* – *lain*. *Laid* needs an object. |
| *Does she ~~likes~~ tea?* | *Does she **like** tea?* | After *does*, use V1. ([Auxiliary verbs](/foundation/f3-auxiliaries/)) |
| *~~Do~~ you ~~be~~ happy?* | ***Are** you happy?* | Main-verb *be* moves itself; no *do*. |

## [Tenses](/topics/t01-tenses/class-11/)

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *I ~~am knowing~~ him.* | *I **know** him.* | *Know* is a stative verb: no continuous form. |
| *She ~~did not went~~.* | *She **did not go**.* | After *did*, use V1. |
| *I ~~have seen~~ him yesterday.* | *I **saw** him yesterday.* | *Yesterday* is a finished time → past simple. |
| *I am here ~~since~~ two years.* | *I **have been** here **for** two years.* | Period → *for*; past to now → present perfect. |
| *If it ~~will rain~~, we'll stay.* | *If it **rains**, we'll stay.* | Present simple after *if / when* for the future. |
| *~~When have you~~ come?* | ***When did** you come?* | *When* asks for a finished time → past simple. |
| *He said that he ~~will~~ come.* | *He said that he **would** come.* | Sequence of tenses after a past main verb. ([Class 12](/topics/t01-tenses/class-12/)) |
| *I ~~have been reading~~ three books.* | *I **have read** three books.* | A number of finished things → present perfect. |
| *She ~~is having~~ two brothers.* | *She **has** two brothers.* | *Have* (= own, be related to) is stative. |

## [Modal auxiliaries](/topics/t02-modal-auxiliaries/class-11/)

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *She can ~~sings~~.* | *She can **sing**.* | Modal + V1. |
| *You must ~~to~~ go.* | *You must **go**.* | No *to* after modals (except *ought to*). |
| *He ~~can able to~~ do it.* | *He **can** / **is able to** do it.* | Use one, not both. |
| *I ~~will can~~ come.* | *I **will be able to** come.* | Two modals cannot go together. |
| *You need not ~~to~~ worry.* | *You **need not** worry.* | Modal *need not* + V1. |
| *Yesterday I ~~must~~ go.* | *Yesterday I **had to** go.* | *Must* has no past form for obligation. |
| *You ~~mustn't~~ come; it's a holiday.* | *You **needn't** come.* | Not necessary ≠ not allowed. |
| *He must ~~has~~ gone.* | *He must **have** gone.* | Always *have* after a modal. ([Class 12](/topics/t02-modal-auxiliaries/class-12/)) |
| *She ~~mustn't have~~ seen me.* | *She **can't have** seen me.* | Negative past deduction → *can't have*. |

## [Articles](/topics/t03-articles-determiners/class-11/)

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *~~a~~ hour* | ***an** hour* | Silent *h* → vowel sound. |
| *~~an~~ university* | ***a** university* | "You" sound → consonant sound. |
| *~~a~~ MLA* | ***an** MLA* | *M* sounds "em". |
| *~~The~~ honesty is the best policy.* | ***Honesty** is the best policy.* | Abstract noun in general: no article. |
| *She plays ~~veena~~.* | *She plays **the** veena.* | Instruments take *the*. |
| *They play ~~the~~ cricket.* | *They play **cricket**.* | Games take no article. |
| *My father is ~~engineer~~.* | *My father is **an** engineer.* | A job needs *a / an*. |
| *He gave me ~~an~~ advice.* | *He gave me **some** advice.* | *Advice* is uncountable. |

## [Determiners](/topics/t03-articles-determiners/class-12/)

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *~~much~~ students* | ***many** students* | Countable → *many*. |
| *~~Every of~~ the boys* | ***Every one of** / **Each of** the boys* | *Every* cannot take *of* directly. |
| *a bangle on ~~every~~ wrist* | *a bangle on **each** wrist* | Two things → *each*. |
| *~~Both~~ the three girls* | ***All three** girls* | *Both* = two only. |
| *Give me ~~few~~ minutes.* | *Give me **a few** minutes.* | *A few* = some (positive meaning). |
| *I have very ~~less~~ friends.* | *I have very **few** friends.* | Countable → *few / fewer*. |
| *~~The two first~~ chapters* | ***The first two** chapters* | Ordinal before cardinal. |
| *The dog wagged ~~it's~~ tail.* | *The dog wagged **its** tail.* | *Its* = possessive; *it's* = it is. |

## [Prepositions](/topics/t04-prepositions/class-11/)

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *~~on~~ 6 o'clock* | ***at** 6 o'clock* | Exact time → *at*. |
| *~~on~~ next Sunday* | ***next** Sunday* | No preposition before *next / last / this / every*. |
| *reached ~~to~~ Madurai* | *reached Madurai* | *Reach* takes no preposition. |
| *discuss ~~about~~ the plan* | *discuss the plan* | *Discuss* takes no preposition. |
| *divide ~~between~~ five children* | *divide **among** five children* | More than two, as a group → *among*. |
| *~~Beside~~ maths, he likes science.* | ***Besides** maths, ...* | In addition to → *besides*. |
| *between you and ~~I~~* | *between you and **me*** | Object pronoun after a preposition. |
| *good ~~in~~ maths* | *good **at** maths* | Fixed partner. ([Class 12](/topics/t04-prepositions/class-12/)) |
| *prefer tea ~~than~~ coffee* | *prefer tea **to** coffee* | *Prefer* takes *to*. |
| *~~Despite of~~ the rain* | ***Despite** the rain* | *Despite* has no *of*. |
| *married ~~with~~ a doctor* | *married **to** a doctor* | Fixed partner. |
| *look forward to ~~meet~~ you* | *look forward to **meeting** you* | *To* is a preposition here → V4. |

## [Subject–verb agreement](/topics/t05-subject-verb-agreement/class-12/)

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *The box of mangoes ~~are~~ heavy.* | *... **is** heavy.* | The subject is *box*. |
| *Bread and butter ~~are~~ my breakfast.* | *... **is** my breakfast.* | One dish → singular. |
| *Neither Ravi nor his friends ~~was~~ there.* | *... **were** there.* | Agree with the nearer subject. |
| *The teacher, along with the students, ~~are~~ here.* | *... **is** here.* | *Along with* does not join subjects. |
| *Each of the girls ~~have~~ a book.* | *... **has** a book.* | *Each* → singular. |
| *The news ~~are~~ good.* | *The news **is** good.* | *News* is singular. |
| *The police ~~has~~ come.* | *The police **have** come.* | *Police* is always plural. |
| *The number of cars ~~are~~ rising.* | *... **is** rising.* | *The number* → singular. |
| *There ~~is~~ many reasons.* | *There **are** many reasons.* | Agree with the noun after *there*. |

## [Connectives and linkers](/topics/t06-connectives/class-12/)

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *Although he is poor, ~~but~~ he is honest.* | *Although he is poor, he is honest.* | One linker for one connection. |
| *Because he was ill, ~~so~~ he stayed home.* | *Because he was ill, he stayed home.* | Same: one linker only. |
| *~~Despite~~ it was cold, we went.* | ***Although** it was cold, ...* / ***Despite the cold**, ...* | *Despite* + noun, not a clause. |
| *Both Ravi ~~as well as~~ Arun* | ***Both** Ravi **and** Arun* | *Both* pairs with *and*. |
| *No sooner had he left ~~when~~ ...* | *No sooner had he left **than** ...* | *No sooner ... than*; *hardly ... when*. |
| *lest you ~~should not~~ fall* | *lest you **should** fall* | *Lest* already means "so that ... not". |
| *It was late, ~~however~~ we continued.* | *It was late; **however,** we continued.* | A linking adverb needs a semicolon or full stop before it. |
| *He was ~~so~~ a good player that ...* | *He was **such** a good player that ...* | *Such* + *a* + adjective + noun. |

## [Non-finite verbs](/topics/t07-non-finite-verbs/class-12/)

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *I enjoy ~~to read~~.* | *I enjoy **reading**.* | *Enjoy* + gerund. |
| *He suggested ~~to go~~.* | *He suggested **going**.* | *Suggest* + gerund (or *that ... should*). |
| *She decided ~~going~~.* | *She decided **to go**.* | *Decide* + *to*-infinitive. |
| *fond of ~~to swim~~* | *fond of **swimming*** | Gerund after a preposition. |
| *made us ~~to~~ stand* | *made us **stand*** | *Make* (active) + bare infinitive. |
| *~~Walking along the road, a snake bit him.~~* | *Walking along the road, **he** was bitten by a snake.* | Dangling participle: the snake was not walking. |
| *I am ~~interesting~~ in music.* | *I am **interested** in music.* | *-ed* = the person who feels. |

## [Degrees of comparison](/topics/t08-degrees-of-comparison/class-12/)

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *~~more better~~* | ***better*** | Already comparative. |
| *the ~~most cleverest~~* | *the **cleverest*** | One superlative form only. |
| *~~elder than~~ me* | ***older than** me* | *Elder* never takes *than*. |
| *larger ~~than any city~~ in India* (Mumbai) | *larger **than any other city** in India* | Mumbai is in the group → *other*. |
| *~~senior than~~ me* | ***senior to** me* | Latin comparatives take *to*. |
| *one of the ~~largest city~~* | *one of the **largest cities*** | *One of the* + plural. |
| *~~very~~ better* | ***much** better* | *Very* doesn't go with comparatives. |
| *cooler than ~~Chennai~~* (comparing climates) | *cooler than **that of** Chennai* | Compare like with like. |

## [Kinds of sentences and transformation](/topics/t09-kinds-of-sentences/kinds-of-sentences/)

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *Unless you do ~~not~~ work, you will fail.* | *Unless you work, you will fail.* | *Unless* = *if not*. |
| *He is ~~too~~ weak ~~that~~ he cannot walk.* | *He is **so** weak **that** he cannot walk.* | *Too ... to*; *so ... that*. |
| *The box is so heavy that I cannot lift.* | *... that I cannot lift **it**.* | *Lift* needs an object. |
| *~~Being a rainy day~~, we stayed in.* | ***It being** a rainy day, ...* / ***As it was** a rainy day, ...* | The participle needs the right subject. |
| *The man ~~which~~ came here* | *The man **who** came here* | *Who* for people. |
| *My father, ~~that~~ is a doctor, ...* | *My father, **who** is a doctor, ...* | No *that* in a clause with commas. |

## [Interrogatives](/topics/t10-questions/class-12/) and [question tags](/topics/t10-questions/class-11/)

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *~~Where you are going?~~* | *Where **are you** going?* | Helping verb before the subject. |
| *What did he ~~said~~?* | *What did he **say**?* | After *did*, V1. |
| *Who ~~did break~~ the glass?* | *Who **broke** the glass?* | Subject question: no *did*. |
| *Can you tell me where ~~is the station~~?* | *... where **the station is**?* | Indirect question: statement order. |
| *How ~~many~~ money?* | *How **much** money?* | Uncountable → *much*. |
| *You are coming, ~~isn't it~~?* | *You are coming, **aren't you**?* | Tag uses the verb and pronoun of the statement. |
| *I am right, ~~amn't I~~?* | *I am right, **aren't I**?* | Special tag for *I am*. |
| *He hardly studies, ~~doesn't he~~?* | *He hardly studies, **does he**?* | *Hardly* → negative sentence → positive tag. |
| *Let's play, ~~will we~~?* | *Let's play, **shall we**?* | *Let's* → *shall we?* |

## [Active and passive voice](/topics/t11-active-passive/class-11/)

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *The letter was ~~wrote~~.* | *The letter was **written**.* | Passive needs V3. |
| *The work ~~has done~~.* | *The work **has been done**.* | Perfect passive: *has been* + V3. |
| *sung by ~~she~~* | *sung by **her*** | Object form after *by*. |
| *My pen has been stolen ~~by someone~~.* | *My pen has been stolen.* | Leave out *by someone*. |
| *The bus ~~was arrived~~ late.* | *The bus **arrived** late.* | *Arrive* has no object → no passive. |
| *~~Who~~ was this poem written?* | ***By whom** was this poem written?* | *Who* (the doer) → *by whom*. ([Class 12](/topics/t11-active-passive/class-12/)) |
| *His name is known ~~by~~ everyone.* | *... known **to** everyone.* | *Known* takes *to*. |
| *He was made ~~wait~~.* | *He was made **to wait**.* | *Make* in the passive takes *to*. |

## [Reported speech](/topics/t12-reported-speech/class-11/)

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *He ~~said me~~ that ...* | *He **told me** that ...* | *Say* cannot take a person directly. |
| *She ~~told that~~ ...* | *She **said that** ...* | *Told* needs a person. |
| *She asked me where ~~did I live~~.* | *She asked me where **I lived**.* | Statement order in reported questions. |
| *He asked ~~that if~~ I was ready.* | *He asked **if** I was ready.* | No *that* with *if*. |
| *She told me ~~don't~~ go.* | *She told me **not to go**.* | Negative command → *not to*. |
| *He suggested ~~me to go~~.* | *He suggested **that I should go**.* | *Suggest* + *that ... should*. ([Class 12](/topics/t12-reported-speech/class-12/)) |
| *She apologised ~~to be~~ late.* | *She apologised **for being** late.* | *Apologise for* + V4. |
| *congratulated him ~~for~~ his success* | *congratulated him **on** his success* | *Congratulate on*. |

## [Conditional clauses](/topics/t13-conditionals/class-11/)

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *If I ~~would have~~ money, ...* | *If I **had** money, ...* | Type 2: past in the *if* clause. |
| *If I ~~would have known~~, ...* | *If I **had known**, ...* | Type 3: past perfect in the *if* clause. |
| *If I ~~was~~ you, ...* | *If I **were** you, ...* | Fixed form for advice. |
| *If he had come, he ~~would help~~.* | *... he **would have helped**.* | Type 3 main clause: *would have* + V3. |
| *~~Hadn't I~~ seen him, ...* | ***Had I not** seen him, ...* | Inversion uses the full form. ([Class 12](/topics/t13-conditionals/class-12/)) |
| *Take a sweater ~~if~~ it gets cold.* (as a precaution) | *... **in case** it gets cold.* | *In case* = as a precaution. |

## [Phrases, clauses and sentence parts](/foundation/f5-phrase-clause/)

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *Because it was late.* (as a full sentence) | *We went home because it was late.* | A subordinate clause cannot stand alone. |
| *He wants to go* → "two clauses" | **one clause** | *To go* is non-finite. |
| *She is a teacher* → "SVO" | **SVC** | *A teacher* = *she*: a complement. ([Sentence parts](/foundation/f4-sentence-parts/)) |
| *She speaks English ~~good~~.* | *She speaks English **well**.* | Adverb needed for a verb. ([Parts of speech](/foundation/f1-parts-of-speech/)) |
| *This is ~~my~~.* | *This is **mine**.* | No noun after it → possessive pronoun. |
