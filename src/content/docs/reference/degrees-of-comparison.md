## How to form them

| Word | Rule | Positive | Comparative | Superlative |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| one syllable | add *-er, -est* | tall | taller | tallest |
| ends in *-e* | add *-r, -st* | large | larger | largest |
| one vowel + one consonant | double the consonant | big | bigger | biggest |
| two syllables ending in *-y* | *y* → *i*, add *-er, -est* | happy | happier | happiest |
| two or more syllables | *more, most* | beautiful | more beautiful | most beautiful |
| some two-syllable words | either form | clever | cleverer / more clever | cleverest / most clever |
| adverbs ending in *-ly* | *more, most* | quickly | more quickly | most quickly |
| short adverbs | add *-er, -est* | fast | faster | fastest |

## Irregular forms

| Positive | Comparative | Superlative | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| good / well | better | best | |
| bad / badly / ill | worse | worst | |
| little | less | least | amount (uncountable) |
| many / much | more | most | *many* countable, *much* uncountable |
| far | farther / further | farthest / furthest | *farther* = distance only; *further* = distance or "more" |
| old | older / elder | oldest / eldest | *elder, eldest*: family only, before a noun, no *than* |
| late | later / latter | latest / last | *later, latest* = time; *latter, last* = order |

## Latin comparatives (take *to*, not *than*)

| Word | Example |
|---|---|
| senior to | *She is **senior to** me.* |
| junior to | *He is **junior to** his brother.* |
| superior to | *This cloth is **superior to** that.* |
| inferior to | *Cheap goods are **inferior to** good ones.* |
| prior to | ***Prior to** the meeting, ...* (= before) |
| prefer ... to | *I **prefer** tea **to** coffee.* |
| preferable to | *Walking is **preferable to** driving.* |

## Patterns

| Pattern | Degree | Example |
|---|---|---|
| *as* + positive + *as* | equal | *Meena is **as tall as** Priya.* |
| *not so / not as* + positive + *as* | unequal | *Ravi is **not so tall as** Arun.* |
| comparative + *than* | two things | *Chennai is **hotter than** Ooty.* |
| *the* + comparative + *of the two* | two things | *She is **the taller of the two**.* |
| *the* + superlative + *in / of* | one of a group | *He is **the tallest in** the class.* |
| *the* + comparative, *the* + comparative | double | ***The more** you read, **the more** you learn.* |
| comparative + *and* + comparative | gradual change | *It is getting **hotter and hotter**.* |

## Changing the degree (interchange)

### When the superlative is "the one" (no *one of*)

| Degree | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Superlative | *X is **the** + superlative + noun ...* | *Mumbai is **the largest** city in India.* |
| Comparative | *X is + comparative + **than any other** + singular noun ...* | *Mumbai is **larger than any other** city in India.* |
| Positive | ***No other** + singular noun ... is **as** + positive + **as** X.* | ***No other** city in India is **as large as** Mumbai.* |

### When the sentence has *one of the*

| Degree | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Superlative | *X is **one of the** + superlative + plural noun ...* | *Kolkata is **one of the largest** cities in India.* |
| Comparative | *X is + comparative + **than most other** + plural noun ...* | *Kolkata is **larger than most other** cities in India.* |
| Positive | ***Very few** + plural noun ... are **as** + positive + **as** X.* | ***Very few** cities in India are **as large as** Kolkata.* |

### Two things

| Degree | Example |
|---|---|
| Comparative | *Gold is **more precious than** silver.* |
| Positive | *Silver is **not so precious as** gold.* |
| Comparative (opposite word) | *Silver is **less precious than** gold.* / *Arun is **shorter than** Ravi.* |

## Errors to avoid

| Wrong | Right |
|---|---|
| *~~more better~~*, *~~most cleverest~~* | *better*, *cleverest* |
| *He is ~~elder than~~ me.* | *He is **older than** me.* |
| *Mumbai is larger ~~than any city~~ in India.* | *... **than any other city** in India.* |
| *~~No other cities~~ is as large ...* | ***No other city** is as large ...* |
| *She is ~~senior than~~ me.* | *She is **senior to** me.* |
| *the ~~tallest~~ of the two* | *the **taller** of the two* |
| *~~very better~~* | ***much / far** better* |
| *one of the ~~largest city~~* | *one of the **largest cities*** |
| *The climate of Ooty is cooler than ~~Chennai~~.* | *... than **that of** Chennai.* |
