Every tense is made of two parts: **time** (present, past or future) and **aspect** (simple, continuous, perfect or perfect continuous). Three times × four aspects = 12 tenses.

The examples use the verb *write* with the subject *she*.

## Present

| Tense | Formula | Positive | Negative | Question |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Present simple | V1 / V5 | She writes. | She does not write. | Does she write? |
| Present continuous | am / is / are + V4 | She is writing. | She is not writing. | Is she writing? |
| Present perfect | has / have + V3 | She has written. | She has not written. | Has she written? |
| Present perfect continuous | has / have been + V4 | She has been writing. | She has not been writing. | Has she been writing? |

## Past

| Tense | Formula | Positive | Negative | Question |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Past simple | V2 | She wrote. | She did not write. | Did she write? |
| Past continuous | was / were + V4 | She was writing. | She was not writing. | Was she writing? |
| Past perfect | had + V3 | She had written. | She had not written. | Had she written? |
| Past perfect continuous | had been + V4 | She had been writing. | She had not been writing. | Had she been writing? |

## Future

| Tense | Formula | Positive | Negative | Question |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Future simple | will / shall + V1 | She will write. | She will not write. | Will she write? |
| Future continuous | will / shall be + V4 | She will be writing. | She will not be writing. | Will she be writing? |
| Future perfect | will / shall have + V3 | She will have written. | She will not have written. | Will she have written? |
| Future perfect continuous | will / shall have been + V4 | She will have been writing. | She will not have been writing. | Will she have been writing? |

## Remember

- In the negative and question forms of the present simple and past simple, the main verb goes back to **V1**: *She does not **write***, *Did she **write**?* (not *~~Did she wrote?~~*).
- *Shall* is used with *I* and *we* in formal or older British English. *Will* is correct with every subject.
