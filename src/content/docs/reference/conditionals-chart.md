A conditional sentence has an ***if* clause** (the condition) and a **main clause** (the result). When the *if* clause comes first, put a comma after it.

Full lessons: [Conditional clauses (Class 11)](/topics/t13-conditionals/class-11/) and [Conditional clauses (Class 12)](/topics/t13-conditionals/class-12/).

## The four types

| Type | Use | *If* clause | Main clause | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0** | general truths, facts, habits | present simple | present simple | *If you **heat** ice, it **melts**.* |
| **1** | real, possible future | present simple | *will* + V1 (or *can, may, must*, imperative) | *If it **rains**, we **will stay** home.* |
| **2** | unreal now / unlikely future | past simple (*were* for all persons) | *would* + V1 (or *could, might*) | *If I **were** rich, I **would travel**.* |
| **3** | unreal past (the opposite happened) | past perfect (*had* + V3) | *would have* + V3 (or *could have, might have*) | *If I **had studied**, I **would have passed**.* |

**The step-back pattern:** each type moves the verbs one step further into the past, as the condition becomes less real.

## Mixed conditionals

| Condition | Result | *If* clause | Main clause | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| past | present | past perfect | *would* + V1 | *If I **had taken** the job, I **would be** rich now.* |
| present (general) | past | past simple | *would have* + V3 | *If I **were** braver, I **would have spoken** up yesterday.* |

## *Unless* and *if ... not*

| With *if ... not* | With *unless* |
|---|---|
| *If you **don't** hurry, you will miss the bus.* | ***Unless** you **hurry**, you will miss the bus.* |
| *If it **doesn't** rain, the crops will die.* | ***Unless** it **rains**, the crops will die.* |
| *You can't enter if you **don't have** a ticket.* | *You can't enter **unless** you **have** a ticket.* |

- *Unless* = *if ... not*. Use a **positive** verb after it. Never *~~unless ... not~~*.
- *Unless* is used mainly in Type 1. It is not used when *if not* gives the cause of a feeling: *I'll be surprised **if** he doesn't come* (not *unless*).

## Other condition words

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| *provided (that), providing, as long as, on condition that* | only if | *You can go **provided** you finish your work.* |
| *in case* | as a precaution (because it may happen) | *Take an umbrella **in case** it rains.* |
| *in case of* + noun | if there is | ***In case of** fire, use the stairs.* |
| *suppose, supposing* | imagine that | ***Supposing** you won, what would you do?* |
| *otherwise, or (else)* | if not | *Hurry, **otherwise** you'll be late.* |
| *even if* | whether or not | ***Even if** it rains, we'll go.* |
| *but for* + noun | without | ***But for** your help, I would have failed.* |
| *if only* | a wish or regret | ***If only** I had listened!* |
| *whether ... or not* | in either case | *I'll go **whether** it rains **or not**.* |

## Inversion (formal, without *if*)

| Type | With *if* | Without *if* |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | *If you **should** need help, call me.* / *If you need help, ...* | ***Should you** need help, call me.* |
| 2 (*be*) | *If I **were** you, I would accept.* | ***Were I** you, I would accept.* |
| 2 (other verbs) | *If he **worked** harder, he would succeed.* | ***Were he to work** harder, he would succeed.* |
| 3 | *If I **had** known, I would have come.* | ***Had I** known, I would have come.* |
| 3 (negative) | *If I **had not** seen it, ...* | ***Had I not** seen it, ...* (never *~~Hadn't I~~*) |
| "without" (present) | *If it **were not** for you, ...* | ***Were it not** for you, ...* / ***But for** you, ...* |
| "without" (past) | *If it **had not been** for you, ...* | ***Had it not been** for you, ...* / ***But for** you, ...* |

Only ***had*, *were*** and ***should*** can be inverted this way.

## The errors to avoid

| Wrong | Right |
|---|---|
| *If it ~~will rain~~, ...* | *If it **rains**, ...* |
| *If I ~~would have~~ money, ...* | *If I **had** money, ...* |
| *If I ~~would have known~~, ...* | *If I **had known**, ...* |
| *If I ~~was~~ you, ...* | *If I **were** you, ...* |
| *If he had come, he ~~would help~~.* | *If he had come, he **would have helped**.* |
| *Unless you ~~don't~~ study, ...* | *Unless you **study**, ...* |
| *~~Hadn't I~~ seen him, ...* | ***Had I not** seen him, ...* |
