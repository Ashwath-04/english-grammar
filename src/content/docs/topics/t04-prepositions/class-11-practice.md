## Part A: Prepositions of time

**1.** *The class begins ______ 9 o'clock.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***at.*** An exact time takes *at*.

</details>

**2.** *Pongal falls ______ January.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***in.*** Months take *in*.

</details>

**3.** *My birthday is ______ 12 May.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***on.*** Dates take *on*.

</details>

**4.** *We will visit Ooty ______ next month.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**No preposition.** **Trap:** *next, last, this* and *every* take no preposition. *We will visit Ooty next month.*

</details>

**5.** *I have been waiting here ______ two hours.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***for.*** *Two hours* is a period of time.

</details>

**6.** *She has been ill ______ Monday.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***since.*** *Monday* is the starting point, and the tense is present perfect.

</details>

**7.** *The lights went off ______ the film.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***during.*** It tells **when** something happened, within the time of an event (the film).

</details>

**8.** *Please return the book ______ a week.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***within.*** "Before one week is over". (*in a week* is also used, but *within* makes the limit clear.)

</details>

**9.** *You must reach the hall ______ 8.30.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***by.*** A deadline for a single action (*reach*): 8.30 or earlier.

</details>

**10.** *I will stay at my aunt's house ______ Sunday.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***until*** (or ***till***). *Stay* continues up to Sunday and then stops. *By* is wrong here, because *stay* is not a single action.

</details>

**11.** *We go for a walk ______ the evening and sleep early ______ night.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***in*** ... ***at.*** *In the evening* (a part of the day), but *at night* (a fixed phrase).

</details>

## Part B: Prepositions of place and movement

**12.** *My uncle lives ______ Coimbatore.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***in.*** Cities take *in*.

</details>

**13.** *Wait for me ______ the bus stop.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***at.*** A bus stop is a point, a place where we meet.

</details>

**14.** *There is a beautiful painting ______ the wall.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***on.*** A wall is a surface.

</details>

**15.** *The children ran ______ the classroom and sat down at their desks.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***into.*** Movement from outside to inside. (*in* would describe position, not movement.)

</details>

**16.** *The train passed ______ a long tunnel.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***through.*** Inside something with sides, in at one end and out at the other.

</details>

**17.** *We swam ______ the river to the other side.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***across.*** From one side to the other side.

</details>

**18.** *A plane flew ______ our house.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***over.*** Higher than the house and moving to the other side. (*above* is also possible, but *over* is best with movement.)

</details>

**19.** *We walked ______ the beach in the evening.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***along.*** Following the line of the beach.

</details>

## Part C: Choose the correct preposition

**20.** *Divide these mangoes ______ the three sisters.* (between / among)

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***among.*** More than two people, as a group.

</details>

**21.** *Kavya sat ______ her best friend.* (beside / besides)

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***beside.*** It means "next to".

</details>

**22.** *______ Tamil, Ravi knows Hindi and English.* (Beside / Besides)

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***Besides.*** It means "in addition to Tamil".

</details>

**23.** *He cut the cake ______ a knife.* (by / with)

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***with.*** A tool or instrument takes *with*.

</details>

**24.** *This poem was written ______ Bharathiyar.* (by / with)

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***by.*** The doer of an action in the passive takes *by*.

</details>

**25.** *The agreement was signed ______ India, Nepal and Bhutan.* (between / among)

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***between.*** **Trap:** there are three countries, but each one is a separate party to the agreement. *Between* is used for separate, individual members, even when there are more than two.

</details>

## Part D: Rewrite or correct

**26.** *Replace the clause with a prepositional phrase: We reached home **when the sun set**.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*We reached home **at sunset**.* The clause tells the time. The noun *sunset* + the time preposition *at*.

</details>

**27.** *Replace the clause with a prepositional phrase: The man **who has a beard** is my uncle.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*The man **with a beard** is my uncle.* *With* + noun can describe a person, like the clause *who has*.

</details>

**28.** *Correct the error: We reached to Chennai late at night.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*We **reached Chennai** late at night.* *Reach* is followed directly by the place, with no preposition. (Compare: *We arrived **in** Chennai*.)

</details>

**29.** *Correct the error: Let us discuss about the problem.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*Let us **discuss the problem**.* *Discuss* takes no preposition. (But the noun takes one: *a discussion **about** the problem*.)

</details>

**30.** *Correct the error: I was born in 15 August.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*I was born **on** 15 August.* Dates take *on*. (*In August* would be correct without the date.)

</details>

**31.** *Correct the error: She has been living here since five years.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*She has been living here **for** five years.* *Five years* is a period → *for*.

</details>
