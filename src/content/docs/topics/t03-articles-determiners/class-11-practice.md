Work through each question, then open the answer to check. Every answer explains **why**, so read it even when you got the question right. Where no article is needed, the answer says **no article** (✗).

## Part A: *a* or *an*?

**1.** *I waited for ______ hour.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***an.*** The *h* in *hour* is silent, so the word begins with a vowel sound ("our").

</details>

**2.** *Divya studies at ______ university in Chennai.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***a.*** **Trap:** *university* begins with the letter *u*, but the sound is "you", which is a consonant sound.

</details>

**3.** *Our new neighbour is ______ MLA.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***an.*** **Trap:** *M* is a consonant letter, but it is read as "em", which begins with a vowel sound.

</details>

**4.** *Can you give me ______ one-rupee coin?*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***a.*** *One* begins with the sound "won" (a *w* sound), which is a consonant sound.

</details>

**5.** *That was ______ honest answer.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***an.*** The *h* in *honest* is silent. The word begins with a vowel sound.

</details>

**6.** *______ European tourist asked us the way to the temple.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***A.*** *European* begins with the letter *E*, but the sound is "you", which is a consonant sound.

</details>

**7.** *The doctor asked for ______ X-ray of my arm.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***an.*** *X* is read as "ex", which begins with a vowel sound.

</details>

**8.** *This is ______ useful book for the exam.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***a.*** *Useful* begins with the sound "you", a consonant sound.

</details>

**9.** *We met ______ old man at the bus stand.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***an.*** The article goes with the sound of the **next word**, which is *old* (a vowel sound), not *man*.

</details>

**10.** *There is ______ hospital near our house.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***a.*** The *h* in *hospital* is sounded, so it is a consonant sound.

</details>

## Part B: Fill in with *a, an, the* or no article (✗)

**11.** *______ Kaveri flows through Tamil Nadu.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***The.*** Names of rivers take *the*.

</details>

**12.** *Mount Everest is ______ highest peak in the world.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***the.*** A superlative (*highest*) takes *the*. (Note that *Mount Everest* itself takes no article.)

</details>

**13.** *My brother is ______ engineer in Coimbatore.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***an.*** A job after *is* needs *a / an*. *Engineer* begins with a vowel sound.

</details>

**14.** *Kavya plays ______ veena beautifully.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***the.*** Musical instruments take *the*.

</details>

**15.** *The boys are playing ______ cricket in the ground.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**No article (✗).** Games take no article. Compare with question 14: *play **the** veena* but *play **cricket***.

</details>

**16.** *______ sun sets in ______ west.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***The*** ... ***the.*** The sun is unique. Directions (*the west*) also take *the*.

</details>

**17.** *______ poor need our help.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***The.*** *The* + adjective means a whole group of people (*the poor* = poor people). The verb is plural (*need*).

</details>

**18.** *We have ______ breakfast at seven.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**No article (✗).** Meals in general take no article.

</details>

**19.** *Ravi was taken to ______ hospital after the accident. His friends went to ______ hospital to see him.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**No article (✗)** ... ***the.*** Ravi went as a patient (the main purpose of a hospital) → no article. His friends went to visit the building → *the*. (American English uses *the hospital* in both places.)

</details>

**20.** *______ Andaman Islands are in ______ Bay of Bengal.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***The*** ... ***the.*** Groups of islands take *the*. Seas and bays take *the*.

</details>

**21.** *______ honesty is ______ best policy.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**No article (✗)** ... ***the.*** An abstract noun in a general sense takes no article. *Best* is a superlative → *the*.

</details>

**22.** *______ honesty of this boy surprised us all.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***The.*** **Trap:** compare with question 21. Here it is a particular honesty (*of this boy*), so it takes *the*.

</details>

**23.** *I bought ______ umbrella yesterday. ______ umbrella is already broken.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***an*** ... ***The.*** First mention → *a / an*; *umbrella* begins with a vowel sound → *an*. Second mention → *the*.

</details>

**24.** *My cousin lives in ______ USA, and my uncle lives in ______ Japan.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***the*** ... **no article (✗).** *The USA* (the United States) contains *States*, so it takes *the*. Most single country names, like *Japan*, take no article.

</details>

**25.** *Petrol costs 105 rupees ______ litre.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***a.*** *A* can mean "per": *105 rupees a litre* = 105 rupees per litre.

</details>

**26.** *What ______ beautiful kolam!*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***a.*** An exclamation with *What* + a singular countable noun needs *a / an*.

</details>

**27.** *______ Tamil is one of ______ oldest languages in ______ world.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**No article (✗)** ... ***the*** ... ***the.*** Languages take no article. *Oldest* is a superlative → *the*. *The world* is unique → *the*.

</details>

## Part C: Find and correct the mistake

**28.** *My teacher gave me an advice.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*My teacher gave me **some advice*** (or ***a piece of advice***). *Advice* is uncountable, so it cannot take *a / an*.

</details>

**29.** *She is a honest girl.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*She is **an** honest girl.* *Honest* begins with a vowel sound (the *h* is silent).

</details>

**30.** *The gold is a precious metal.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***Gold** is a precious metal.* A material noun used in general takes no article.

</details>

**31.** *We travelled to Madurai by the train.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*We travelled to Madurai **by train**.* *By* + a means of transport is a fixed phrase with no article.

</details>

**32.** *They climbed the Mount Everest in 2010.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*They climbed **Mount Everest** in 2010.* A single mountain takes no article. (Mountain **ranges**, like *the Himalayas*, take *the*.)

</details>

**33.** *My father is engineer.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*My father is **an** engineer.* A job after *be* needs *a / an*. This is a very common mistake.

</details>
