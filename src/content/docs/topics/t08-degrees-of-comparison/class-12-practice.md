## Part A: Fill in with the correct form of the word in brackets

**1.** *Summer in Chennai is ______ (hot) than summer in Ooty.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***hotter.*** *Than* shows a comparative. *Hot* (one vowel + one consonant) doubles the *t*.

</details>

**2.** *This is the ______ (interesting) book I have ever read.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***most interesting.*** *The* + a comparison with all the books → superlative. A long word takes *most*.

</details>

**3.** *Today is ______ (bad) than yesterday. It is raining even more.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***worse.*** *Bad – worse – worst* is irregular. (*badder* does not exist.)

</details>

**4.** *Of the two sisters, Divya is the ______ (clever).*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***cleverer*** (or ***more clever***). **Trap:** *the* + *of the two* takes the **comparative**, not the superlative, because there are only two people.

</details>

**5.** *Karthik is my ______ (old) brother.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***elder.*** A family member, before a noun, with no *than* → *elder*. (*older brother* is also correct; *elder* is the traditional choice.)

</details>

**6.** *My brother is three years ______ (old) than me.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***older.*** **Trap:** with *than*, use *older*, never *elder*.

</details>

**7.** *For ______ (far) information, please contact the office.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***further.*** It means "more, additional". *Farther* is used only for distance.

</details>

**8.** *Have you heard the ______ (late) news about the cyclone?*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***latest.*** It means "the newest" (time). *Last* would mean "the final one".

</details>

**9.** *She came ______ (late) than everyone else.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***later.*** A comparison of time with *than*. (*Latter* means "the second of two".)

</details>

**10.** *Meena is as ______ (tall) as her mother.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***tall.*** Between *as ... as*, always use the positive degree.

</details>

**11.** *This cloth is superior ______ that one.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***to.*** *Superior* is a Latin comparative and takes *to*, not *than*.

</details>

## Part B: Change the degree without changing the meaning

**12.** *Change into comparative and positive: Mount Everest is the highest mountain in the world.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

- **Comparative:** *Mount Everest is **higher than any other** mountain in the world.*
- **Positive:** ***No other** mountain in the world is **as high as** Mount Everest.*

*Than any other* and *no other* take a singular noun (*mountain*).

</details>

**13.** *Change into comparative and positive: Arun is the most intelligent student in the class.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

- **Comparative:** *Arun is **more intelligent than any other** student in the class.*
- **Positive:** ***No other** student in the class is **as intelligent as** Arun.*

</details>

**14.** *Change into comparative and positive: Sachin was one of the greatest batsmen in the world.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

- **Comparative:** *Sachin was **greater than most other** batsmen in the world.*
- **Positive:** ***Very few** batsmen in the world were **as great as** Sachin.*

**Trap:** *one of the* means "among the best", not "the best". So use *most other* and *very few*, not *any other* and *no other*.

</details>

**15.** *Change into superlative and comparative: No other city in Tamil Nadu is as big as Chennai.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

- **Superlative:** *Chennai is **the biggest** city in Tamil Nadu.*
- **Comparative:** *Chennai is **bigger than any other** city in Tamil Nadu.*

</details>

**16.** *Change into superlative and positive: Madurai is older than most other cities in South India.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

- **Superlative:** *Madurai is **one of the oldest** cities in South India.*
- **Positive:** ***Very few** cities in South India are **as old as** Madurai.*

*Most other* goes back to *one of the* + plural noun.

</details>

**17.** *Change into superlative and comparative: Very few students are as good as Priya at debate.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

- **Superlative:** *Priya is **one of the best** students at debate.*
- **Comparative:** *Priya is **better** at debate **than most other** students.*

*Very few* goes with *one of the* and *most other*. *Good – better – best*.

</details>

**18.** *Change into positive: Gold is more precious than silver.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*Silver is **not so precious as** gold.* (or *not as precious as*.) Two things: swap the order and use *not so ... as* with the positive.

</details>

**19.** *Change into comparative: Ravi is not so strong as his brother.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*His brother is **stronger than** Ravi.* (or *Ravi is **weaker than** his brother*.) Swap the order and use the comparative.

</details>

**20.** *Change into comparative and positive: Priya runs the fastest in her class.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

- **Comparative:** *Priya runs **faster than any other** girl in her class.* (or *any other student*.)
- **Positive:** ***No other** girl in her class runs **as fast as** Priya.*

*Fastest* is an adverb here (it describes *runs*). Adverbs change degree in the same way.

</details>

**21.** *Change into superlative and comparative: No other festival in Tamil Nadu is so popular as Pongal.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

- **Superlative:** *Pongal is **the most popular** festival in Tamil Nadu.*
- **Comparative:** *Pongal is **more popular than any other** festival in Tamil Nadu.*

</details>

**22.** *Change into positive: The Taj Mahal is more beautiful than any building in Chennai.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***No building** in Chennai is **as beautiful as** the Taj Mahal.* **Trap:** the Taj Mahal is not in Chennai, so the sentence correctly uses *any* without *other*. In the positive, use *no building*, not *no other building*.

</details>

**23.** *Change into comparative: Kavya is as tall as Meena.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*Meena is **not taller than** Kavya.* (or *Kavya is **no shorter than** Meena*.) When two things are equal, the comparative form uses *not* + comparative.

</details>

## Part C: Find and correct the mistake

**24.** *This mango is more sweeter than that one.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*This mango is **sweeter** than that one.* Use either *-er* or *more*, never both.

</details>

**25.** *He is the most cleverest boy in the school.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*He is the **cleverest** boy in the school.* (or *the **most clever***.) Use one superlative form only.

</details>

**26.** *My sister is elder than me.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*My sister is **older** than me.* *Elder* is never followed by *than*.

</details>

**27.** *Chennai is larger than any city in Tamil Nadu.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*Chennai is larger than **any other** city in Tamil Nadu.* Chennai is a city in Tamil Nadu, so we need *other*. Without it, Chennai is compared with itself.

</details>

**28.** *She is senior than all of us.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*She is senior **to** all of us.* *Senior* takes *to*.

</details>

**29.** *No other boys in the team is as fast as Arun.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***No other boy** in the team is as fast as Arun.* *No other* takes a singular noun.

</details>

**30.** *Coimbatore is one of the cleanest city in India.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*Coimbatore is one of the cleanest **cities** in India.* *One of the* + plural noun.

</details>

**31.** *The climate of Ooty is cooler than Chennai.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*The climate of Ooty is cooler than **that of** Chennai.* We must compare climate with climate, not climate with a city.

</details>

**32.** *Your answer is very better than mine.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*Your answer is **much** better than mine.* (or *far better*.) *Very* is not used with comparatives.

</details>
