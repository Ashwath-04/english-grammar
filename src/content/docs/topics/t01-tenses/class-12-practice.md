## Part A: Fill in with the correct tense form

**1.** *I ______ (finish) my homework an hour ago.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***finished.*** *An hour ago* is a finished past time → past simple. The present perfect cannot go with *ago*.

</details>

**2.** *I ______ (already finish) my homework. You can check it now.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***have already finished.*** No finished time is given, and the result matters now (*you can check it now*) → present perfect. *Already* goes between *have* and V3.

</details>

**3.** *Bharathiyar ______ (write) many patriotic songs.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***wrote.*** The poet is no longer alive, so his life is a finished period → past simple. *Has written* would suggest he can still write more.

</details>

**4.** *My uncle ______ (work) in Dubai for five years. Now he lives in Chennai again.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***worked.*** **Trap:** *for five years* makes you think of the present perfect. But the work in Dubai is over (*now he lives in Chennai*), so use the past simple.

</details>

**5.** *My aunt ______ (work) in Dubai for five years, and she still enjoys her job there.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***has worked*** or ***has been working.*** She started five years ago and still works there → present perfect (continuous). Compare with question 4.

</details>

**6.** *When I reached the cinema, the film ______ (start), so I missed the first scene.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***had started.*** The film started **before** I reached. That order matters (I missed a scene) → past perfect.

</details>

**7.** *Amma ______ (paint) the walls all morning. Her hands are covered in paint.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***has been painting.*** An activity over a period (*all morning*) that explains what we see now (paint on her hands) → present perfect continuous.

</details>

**8.** *Amma ______ (paint) three rooms so far.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***has painted.*** **Trap:** a **number** of finished things (*three rooms*) takes the present perfect, not the continuous. *~~has been painting three rooms~~* is wrong.

</details>

**9.** *______ you ever ______ (eat) jigarthanda in Madurai?*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***Have*** ... ***eaten.*** *Ever* asks about experience up to now → present perfect. *Eat – ate – eaten*.

</details>

**10.** *When ______ you ______ (buy) this phone?*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***did*** ... ***buy.*** *When* asks for a finished time → past simple. *~~When have you bought~~* is wrong.

</details>

**11.** *This is the first time I ______ (travel) by plane.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***have travelled.*** *This is the first time* is followed by the present perfect. (American spelling: *traveled*.)

</details>

**12.** *I will call you after I ______ (reach) home.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***reach*** or ***have reached.*** After a time word like *after*, use the present simple or the present perfect for the future. Never *will reach*.

</details>

**13.** *It is three years since I last ______ (see) my cousin.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***saw.*** In the pattern *It is* + period + *since*, the *since* clause takes the past simple.

</details>

**14.** *No sooner ______ the bell ______ (ring) than the students rushed out.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***had*** ... ***rung.*** *No sooner* takes the past perfect, with inversion (*had* before the subject). The bell rang first. *Ring – rang – rung*.

</details>

**15.** *Our geography teacher told us that the earth ______ (move) round the sun.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***moves.*** **Trap:** the main verb *told* is past, but this is a scientific fact. Universal truths stay in the present simple.

</details>

**16.** *Ravi said that he ______ (can) not come to the party.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***could.*** Sequence of tenses: past main verb (*said*) → *can* becomes *could*.

</details>

**17.** *I ______ (know) Divya since we ______ (be) in primary school.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***have known*** ... ***were.*** The main clause with *since* takes the present perfect. *Know* is stative, so not *have been knowing*. The *since* clause takes the past simple.

</details>

**18.** *She hasn't replied to my message ______.* (already / yet / just)

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***yet.*** *Yet* is used in negatives and questions, at the end of the clause.

</details>

**19.** *The guests have ______ arrived. Go and welcome them.* (yet / just / ever)

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***just.*** *Just* means "a very short time ago", which fits *go and welcome them*. *Yet* is not used in positive sentences like this; *ever* is for questions.

</details>

## Part B: Find and correct the mistake

**20.** *I have seen him two days ago.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*I **saw** him two days ago.* *Ago* is a finished time → past simple.

</details>

**21.** *She is ill since two years.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*She **has been** ill **for** two years.* *Two years* is a period → *for*. From the past up to now → present perfect.

</details>

**22.** *He said that he will help me with my project.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*He said that he **would** help me with my project.* Past main verb → *will* becomes *would*.

</details>

**23.** *When have you come back from Trichy?*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*When **did** you **come** back from Trichy?* *When* takes the past simple.

</details>

**24.** *I am working in this office since 2020.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*I **have been working** in this office since 2020.* From a point in the past up to now → present perfect continuous, not present continuous.

</details>

**25.** *I have been writing four letters today.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*I **have written** four letters today.* A number of completed things → present perfect.

</details>

**26.** *He did not went to school because of the rain.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*He did not **go** to school because of the rain.* After *did*, use V1.

</details>

**27.** *I have not never seen snow.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*I have **never** seen snow.* (or *I have **not ever** seen snow*.) *Never* is already negative. Two negatives together are wrong.

</details>

**28.** *The thief ran away before the police arrive.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*The thief ran away before the police **arrived**.* Both actions are in the past, so *arrive* must be past too. (*The thief **had run** away before the police arrived* is also correct.)

</details>

## Part C: Complete the story

Read the story and fill each numbered blank with the correct form of the verb.

> *It ______ (29. be) a hot afternoon in Madurai. Children ______ (30. play) in the street. Suddenly, Meena ______ (31. hear) a loud crash. A big branch ______ (32. fall) on a parked car. Luckily, the owner ______ (33. leave) the car only a few minutes earlier. Since that day, nobody ______ (34. park) under that tree.*

**29.** *(be)*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***was.*** It sets the scene in the past. *Be* is stative, so the past simple is used, not the continuous.

</details>

**30.** *(play)*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***were playing.*** A background action already going on → past continuous. *Children* is plural → *were*.

</details>

**31.** *(hear)*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***heard.*** A main event that moves the story forward → past simple.

</details>

**32.** *(fall)*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***fell.*** The branch fell at the same moment as the crash, so it is the next main event → past simple. (*had fallen* is possible only if Meena saw it later, after it had fallen.)

</details>

**33.** *(leave)*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***had left.*** The owner left **before** the branch fell (*a few minutes earlier*) → past perfect.

</details>

**34.** *(park)*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***has parked.*** *Since that day* means from a past point up to now → present perfect. *Nobody* is singular and already negative, so use *has parked*, not *has not parked*.

</details>
