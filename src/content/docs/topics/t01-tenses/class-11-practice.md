## Part A: Fill in with the correct form of the verb

**1.** *Water ______ (boil) at 100 °C.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***boils.*** A scientific fact → present simple. *Water* is singular → V5.

</details>

**2.** *Listen! Someone ______ (knock) at the door.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***is knocking.*** *Listen!* shows an action going on now → present continuous. *Someone* is singular → *is*.

</details>

**3.** *Arun ______ (visit) Ooty three times so far.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***has visited.*** *So far* means "up to now". Experience up to now → present perfect.

</details>

**4.** *My grandmother ______ (live) in this house for forty years.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***has lived*** or ***has been living.*** She started living there forty years ago and still lives there → present perfect or present perfect continuous. Both are correct.

</details>

**5.** *We ______ (watch) the IPL final last night.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***watched.*** *Last night* is a finished past time → past simple (V2).

</details>

**6.** *While Priya ______ (cook), the power ______ (go) off.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***was cooking*** ... ***went.*** The long action going on (cooking) → past continuous. The short action that interrupted it → past simple.

</details>

**7.** *By the time we reached the theatre, the film ______ (start).*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***had started.*** The film started **before** we reached. The earlier of two past actions → past perfect.

</details>

**8.** *Look at those dark clouds! It ______ (rain).*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***is going to rain.*** A prediction based on something we can see now (the clouds) → *be going to*. (*It will rain* is not wrong, but *going to* is the best choice when there is evidence.)

</details>

**9.** *I ______ (know) the answer now.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***know.*** **Trap:** *now* makes you think of the present continuous, but *know* is a stative verb. It is not used in the continuous. *~~I am knowing~~* is wrong.

</details>

**10.** *The train to Madurai ______ (leave) at 6.15 tomorrow morning.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***leaves.*** A timetable, even about the future → present simple. (*will leave* and *is leaving* are also possible, but the present simple is the expected answer for a timetable.)

</details>

**11.** *If it ______ (rain) tomorrow, the match will be cancelled.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***rains.*** **Trap:** *tomorrow* is the future, but after *if* we use the present simple, not *will rain*.

</details>

**12.** *At this time tomorrow, we ______ (travel) to Kanyakumari.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***will be travelling.*** An action that will be in progress at a time in the future → future continuous. (American spelling: *traveling*.)

</details>

**13.** *By next March, Kavya ______ (complete) her course.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***will have completed.*** Finished **before** a future time (*by next March*) → future perfect.

</details>

**14.** *Karthik ______ (play) for three hours when it started to rain.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***had been playing.*** An action that went on **for a period** (three hours) before another past action (it started to rain) → past perfect continuous.

</details>

**15.** *By 2030, my father ______ (work) in this bank for twenty-five years.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***will have been working.*** A future point (*by 2030*) and a period (*for twenty-five years*) → future perfect continuous. (*will have worked* is also accepted.)

</details>

**16.** *Meena ______ (think) about joining a dance class these days.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***is thinking.*** **Trap:** *think* is usually stative, but here it means "considering", which is an action. Action meaning + *these days* → present continuous.

</details>

**17.** *I ______ (think) you are right.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***think.*** Here *think* means "believe", which is a stative meaning. Use the simple form. Compare with question 16.

</details>

**18.** *Ravi isn't at home. He ______ (go) to the market.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***has gone.*** **Trap:** *has been* would mean he went and came back. He is not at home, so he is still at the market → *has gone*.

</details>

## Part B: Identify the tense

**19.** *The children **have been playing** since noon.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Present perfect continuous.** *Have* (present) + *been* + V4.

</details>

**20.** *She **had left** before I arrived.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Past perfect.** *Had* (past) + V3.

</details>

**21.** *We **will be waiting** for you at the gate.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Future continuous.** *Will* (future) + *be* + V4.

</details>

**22.** *Kavya **writes** poems in Tamil and English.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Present simple.** No helping verb, and the verb is V5.

</details>

**23.** *They **were watching** TV when I called.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Past continuous.** *Were* (past) + V4.

</details>

**24.** *I **shall have finished** the book by then.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Future perfect.** *Shall* (future) + *have* + V3. *Shall* with *I* is formal British English; it is the same tense as *will have finished*.

</details>

## Part C: Find and correct the mistake

**25.** *I am knowing her for ten years.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*I **have known** her for ten years.* *Know* is stative (no continuous). An action that started in the past and is still true needs the present perfect.

</details>

**26.** *She did not came to school yesterday.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*She did not **come** to school yesterday.* After *did*, use V1. *Did* already shows the past.

</details>

**27.** *I have met him last week.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*I **met** him last week.* *Last week* is a finished past time, so use the past simple. The present perfect cannot go with *last week, yesterday* or *ago*.

</details>

**28.** *We are living in Coimbatore since 2015.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*We **have been living** in Coimbatore since 2015.* (or *We **have lived***.) An action from a point in the past (*since 2015*) up to now needs the present perfect (continuous), not the present continuous.

</details>

**29.** *My brother go to the gym every evening.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*My brother **goes** to the gym every evening.* A habit → present simple. *My brother* (he) needs V5.

</details>

**30.** *When I will finish my work, I will call you.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*When I **finish** my work, I will call you.* After *when* (a time word), use the present simple for the future. *Will* stays only in the main clause.

</details>

## Part D: Complete the passage

Read the passage and fill each numbered blank with the correct form of the verb.

> *Divya ______ (31. study) in Class 11 at a school in Salem. Every morning she ______ (32. catch) the 7.30 bus. Yesterday she ______ (33. miss) it because she ______ (34. not hear) her alarm. Since then, she ______ (35. keep) her phone next to her pillow.*

**31.** *(study)*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***studies.*** A present situation that is generally true → present simple, V5 with *Divya*. (*is studying* is also accepted, if we see it as a temporary situation this year.)

</details>

**32.** *(catch)*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***catches.*** *Every morning* shows a habit → present simple. *Catch* ends in *-ch* → add *-es*.

</details>

**33.** *(miss)*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***missed.*** *Yesterday* is a finished past time → past simple.

</details>

**34.** *(not hear)*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***had not heard*** or ***did not hear.*** Not hearing the alarm happened **before** she missed the bus, so the past perfect fits. Because *because* already makes the order clear, the past simple *did not hear* is also correct.

</details>

**35.** *(keep)*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***has kept*** or ***has been keeping.*** *Since then* means from a past point up to now → present perfect (or present perfect continuous).

</details>
