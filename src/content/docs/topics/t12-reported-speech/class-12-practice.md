## Part A: Mixed sentences

**1.** *Ravi said, "I am hungry. Is there any food?"*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***Ravi said that he was hungry and asked if there was any food.*** Statement → *said that*; yes/no question → *asked if*. Two verbs, joined by *and*.

</details>

**2.** *The teacher said to the boys, "Stop talking. Why are you so noisy?"*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***The teacher ordered (or told) the boys to stop talking and asked why they were so noisy.*** Command → *ordered ... to*; wh- question → *asked why* + statement order.

</details>

**3.** *Meena said, "It's very hot. Please switch on the fan."*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***Meena said that it was very hot and requested me to switch on the fan.*** (Use the listener's name if it is given.) *Please* → *requested*.

</details>

**4.** *Arun said to me, "Where were you yesterday? I called you twice."*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***Arun asked me where I had been the previous day and added that he had called me twice.*** *Where were you* → *where I had been*; *yesterday → the previous day*; the second statement → *added that*.

</details>

**5.** *She said, "Hurray! I have got the job. Let's celebrate."*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***She exclaimed with joy that she had got the job and suggested that they should celebrate.*** Exclamation → *exclaimed with joy*; *Let's* → *suggested that ... should*.

</details>

**6.** *The old man said, "I am very poor. Please help me."*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***The old man said that he was very poor and begged (or requested) us to help him.*** A desperate request → *begged*.

</details>

**7.** *Divya said to Kavya, "Your dress is lovely. Where did you buy it?"*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***Divya told Kavya that her dress was lovely and asked her where she had bought it.*** Statement → *told ... that*; question → *asked where* + past perfect.

</details>

**8.** *The doctor said to the patient, "You are much better now. Don't eat spicy food."*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***The doctor told the patient that he (or she) was much better then and advised him (or her) not to eat spicy food.*** Negative advice → *advised ... not to*. *Now → then*.

</details>

## Part B: Use a precise reporting verb

**9.** *"I'm sorry I'm late," said Karthik.* (apologised)

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***Karthik apologised for being late.*** *Apologise* + *for* + V4.

</details>

**10.** *"I will return your book tomorrow," Priya said to me.* (promised)

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***Priya promised to return my book the next day.*** (or *Priya promised me that she would return my book the next day.*) *Promise* + *to* + V1.

</details>

**11.** *"No, I will not sign this paper," said the clerk.* (refused)

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***The clerk refused to sign that paper.*** *Refuse* + *to* + V1. *This → that*.

</details>

**12.** *"Yes, I took the money," said the boy.* (admitted)

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***The boy admitted taking (or having taken) the money.*** (or *The boy admitted that he had taken the money.*)

</details>

**13.** *"I didn't copy in the exam," said Arun.* (denied)

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***Arun denied copying (or having copied) in the exam.*** (or *Arun denied that he had copied in the exam.*) **Trap:** *denied* already has a negative meaning, so do not add *not*.

</details>

**14.** *"Don't touch the hot pan," Amma said to me.* (warned)

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***Amma warned me not to touch the hot pan.*** *Warn* + person + *not to* + V1.

</details>

**15.** *"Congratulations on your success!" said the principal to Divya.* (congratulated)

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***The principal congratulated Divya on her success.*** *Congratulate* + person + *on*.

</details>

**16.** *"Thank you for the beautiful gift," said Meena to her aunt.* (thanked)

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***Meena thanked her aunt for the beautiful gift.*** *Thank* + person + *for*.

</details>

**17.** *"You stole my pen!" said Ravi to Arun.* (accused)

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***Ravi accused Arun of stealing (or having stolen) his pen.*** *Accuse* + person + *of* + V4.

</details>

**18.** *"Don't forget to call me," she said to her son.* (reminded)

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***She reminded her son to call her.*** *Remind* + person + *to* + V1. *Don't forget* is shown by the verb *reminded*.

</details>

## Part C: *Let's*, *Shall I* and *Will you*

**19.** *"Let's go to Marina Beach," said Priya to her friends.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***Priya suggested to her friends that they should go to Marina Beach.*** (or *Priya suggested going to Marina Beach.*) *Let's* → *suggested that ... should*.

</details>

**20.** *"Let's not quarrel," said the brothers.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***The brothers suggested that they should not quarrel.*** (or *... agreed not to quarrel*.) Negative *Let's not* → *should not*.

</details>

**21.** *"Shall I carry your bag?" said Arun to the old woman.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***Arun offered to carry the old woman's bag.*** **Trap:** *Shall I ...?* here is an **offer**, not a real question. Report it with *offered to*.

</details>

**22.** *"Shall we start the meeting?" said the chairman.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***The chairman suggested that they should start the meeting.*** *Shall we ...?* is a suggestion.

</details>

**23.** *"Will you please help me with this box?" said Kavya to Ravi.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***Kavya requested Ravi to help her with that box.*** *Will you please ...?* is a request.

</details>

## Part D: Report the dialogue

> *Teacher: "Why didn't you submit your project, Arun?"*
> *Arun: "I'm sorry, sir. I was ill last week."*
> *Teacher: "All right. Submit it tomorrow."*

**24.** *Report the teacher's first question.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***The teacher asked Arun why he had not submitted his project.*** Wh- question → *asked why* + statement order. *Didn't submit* → *had not submitted*.

</details>

**25.** *Report Arun's reply.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***Arun apologised respectfully and explained that he had been ill the previous week.*** *I'm sorry* → *apologised*; *sir* → *respectfully*; *was → had been*; *last week → the previous week* (or *the week before*).

</details>

**26.** *Report the teacher's answer.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***The teacher accepted his reason and told him to submit it the next day.*** *All right* → *accepted / agreed*; command → *told ... to*; *tomorrow → the next day*.

</details>

## Part E: Change into direct speech

**27.** *Ravi told me that he would meet me at the station.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*Ravi said to me, "**I will meet you** at the station."* *Told me → said to me*; *he → I*; *would → will*; *me* (the listener) → *you*.

</details>

**28.** *She asked her brother if he had seen her keys.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*She said to her brother, "**Have you seen my keys?**"* *If* + past perfect → a yes/no question in the present perfect.

</details>

**29.** *The captain ordered his men to attack.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*The captain said to his men, "**Attack!**"* An order → an imperative sentence.

</details>

**30.** *He exclaimed with sorrow that his dog had died.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*He said, "**Alas! My dog has died.**"* (or *My dog is dead.*) *Exclaimed with sorrow* → *Alas!*

</details>

## Part F: Find and correct the mistake

**31.** *He suggested me to see a doctor.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*He suggested **that I should see** a doctor.* (or *He **advised me to** see a doctor.*) *Suggest* is not followed by person + *to*-infinitive.

</details>

**32.** *She apologised me for being rude.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*She apologised **to me** for being rude.* *Apologise* needs *to* before the person.

</details>
