Work through each question, then open the answer to check. Every answer explains **why**, so read it even when you got the question right. Where *if* is used, *whether* is also correct.

If you get stuck, go back to the lesson: [Reported speech (Class 11)](/topics/t12-reported-speech/class-11/). The [Reported speech changes](/reference/reported-speech-changes/) page has all the tables on one page.

## Part A: Statements

**1.** *Arun said, "I am hungry."*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***Arun said that he was hungry.*** *I → he*; *am → was* (present → past).

</details>

**2.** *Meena said to me, "I like your new bag."*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***Meena told me that she liked my new bag.*** *Said to me → told me*. *I → she*; *your → my* (the listener is me); *like → liked*.

</details>

**3.** *Ravi said, "I have finished my homework."*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***Ravi said that he had finished his homework.*** Present perfect → past perfect.

</details>

**4.** *Kavya said, "I saw a snake in the garden yesterday."*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***Kavya said that she had seen a snake in the garden the previous day.*** Past simple → past perfect. *Yesterday → the previous day* (or *the day before*).

</details>

**5.** *My father said, "I will buy you a cycle next month."*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***My father said that he would buy me a cycle the following month.*** *Will → would*; *you → me*; *next month → the following month*.

</details>

**6.** *The boy said, "I can climb this tree."*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***The boy said that he could climb that tree.*** *Can → could*; *this → that*.

</details>

**7.** *Our science teacher said, "The sun rises in the east."*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***Our science teacher said that the sun rises in the east.*** **Trap:** a universal truth keeps the present tense, even after a past reporting verb.

</details>

**8.** *Divya says, "I am going to Madurai."*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***Divya says that she is going to Madurai.*** **Trap:** the reporting verb *says* is in the present, so the tense does not change.

</details>

## Part B: Questions

**9.** *He said to me, "Are you a student?"*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***He asked me if I was a student.*** Yes/no question → *asked if*. Statement order (*I was*). Full stop at the end.

</details>

**10.** *Priya said to Arun, "Do you like cricket?"*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***Priya asked Arun if he liked cricket.*** *Do* is removed; *like → liked*. *You → he* (Arun).

</details>

**11.** *The teacher said to Ravi, "Have you done your homework?"*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***The teacher asked Ravi if he had done his homework.*** Present perfect → past perfect. *Your → his*.

</details>

**12.** *She said to me, "Where do you live?"*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***She asked me where I lived.*** Wh- question → keep *where*. Remove *do*; statement order: *I lived*.

</details>

**13.** *The stranger said, "What is the time?"*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***The stranger asked what the time was.*** Statement order: *the time was* (not *~~what was the time~~*).

</details>

**14.** *Amma said to me, "Why are you crying?"*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***Amma asked me why I was crying.*** Present continuous → past continuous; statement order.

</details>

**15.** *Karthik said to his friend, "Who broke this window?"*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***Karthik asked his friend who had broken that window.*** *Who* is the subject, so the order is already *who* + verb. Past simple → past perfect; *this → that*.

</details>

**16.** *The officer said to me, "Will you come here tomorrow?"*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***The officer asked me if I would go there the next day.*** *Will → would*; *here → there*; *tomorrow → the next day*. *Come* often becomes *go* when *here* becomes *there* (*come* is also accepted).

</details>

## Part C: Commands and requests

**17.** *The teacher said to the students, "Stand up."*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***The teacher told (or ordered) the students to stand up.*** A command → *told / ordered* + person + *to* + V1.

</details>

**18.** *Amma said to me, "Don't play in the sun."*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***Amma told me not to play in the sun.*** Negative command → *not to* + V1.

</details>

**19.** *Meena said to her brother, "Please lend me your pen."*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***Meena requested her brother to lend her his pen.*** *Please* → *requested* (remove *please*). *Me → her*; *your → his*.

</details>

**20.** *The doctor said to the patient, "Avoid oily food."*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***The doctor advised the patient to avoid oily food.*** Advice → *advised*.

</details>

**21.** *The captain said to the soldiers, "March forward!"*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***The captain ordered (or commanded) the soldiers to march forward.*** A command from someone in authority → *ordered / commanded*.

</details>

**22.** *The old woman said to the man, "Please don't leave me alone."*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***The old woman begged (or requested) the man not to leave her alone.*** A humble, desperate request → *begged*. Negative → *not to*.

</details>

## Part D: Exclamations and wishes

**23.** *The children said, "Hurray! We have won the match."*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***The children exclaimed with joy that they had won the match.*** *Hurray* → *with joy*. Present perfect → past perfect.

</details>

**24.** *She said, "Alas! I have failed."*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***She exclaimed with sorrow that she had failed.*** *Alas* → *with sorrow*.

</details>

**25.** *Ravi said, "What a beautiful rainbow!"*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***Ravi exclaimed that it was a very beautiful rainbow.*** *What a ...!* → a statement with *very*.

</details>

**26.** *Grandpa said to me, "May you live long!"*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***Grandpa prayed that I might live long.*** (or *Grandpa blessed me and wished that I might live long*.) A prayer or blessing → *prayed*; *may → might*.

</details>

**27.** *The students said to the teacher, "Good morning, madam."*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

***The students wished the teacher good morning.*** A greeting → *wished* + person + the greeting. *Madam* is removed.

</details>

## Part E: Change into direct speech

**28.** *Arun told me that he was tired.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*Arun said to me, "**I am** tired."* *Told me → said to me*. *He* (Arun, the speaker) → *I*; *was → am*.

</details>

**29.** *She asked me where I was going.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*She said to me, "**Where are you going?**"* Question order comes back (*are you*), with a question mark. *I* (the listener) → *you*.

</details>

**30.** *The teacher ordered the boys not to make a noise.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*The teacher said to the boys, "**Don't make a noise.**"* *Not to* → *Don't*.

</details>

## Part F: Find and correct the mistake

**31.** *He said me that he was busy.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*He **told me** that he was busy.* (or *He said that he was busy*.) *Said* cannot take a person directly.

</details>

**32.** *She asked me where did I buy my phone.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*She asked me where **I had bought** my phone.* Statement order; remove *did*; past simple → past perfect.

</details>

**33.** *The teacher told us that we will have a test.*

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

*The teacher told us that we **would** have a test.* Past reporting verb → *will* becomes *would*.

</details>
